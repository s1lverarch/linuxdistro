Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

SilverArch Linux is an open-source project maintained by Silvercore (s1lverarch). Contact: s1lv3rcore@proton.me.

Data we collect

This website has no user accounts, no contact forms and no analytics. We do not collect personal data by design.

Data stored in your browser

The site saves your chosen language and your chosen button colour in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time from your browser settings.

Third-party services

When you use some features, your browser sends requests to these services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub (api.github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com): repository data, commit history and the package database.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com): fonts used by the site.
  • rss2json and allorigins.win: proxy services used to load the news feed.
  • Phoronix (phoronix.com): news headlines.

These services may log your IP address as part of their normal operation.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs under its own policy.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: s1lv3rcore@proton.me.
